# Decode throughput part of the pie vs vLLM gap on L4 (gpt-oss c8/c16 selects on the run kernel)

gpt-oss-20b's c8 and c16 decodes route 32 and 64 routes over its 32 experts, 1 and 2 an expert, and the L4 profile's `moe.mxfp4_mma_per_from = 4` (copied from the L40S file) kept them off the register-dequant kernels. Being under the 2-an-expert grouping threshold too, they ran the by-route `moe_matmul_select_mxfp4`, which streams an expert's codes once for each route: 0.84 ms a select, about 40 ms of a c16 step whose ITL is 50 ms (nsys). vLLM 0.32.0 runs Marlin at every width. With `= 0`, as on the A40, they take the 512-code run kernel that c25–c128 already decode on, which reads each touched expert once (0.47 ms a select). c17–c24 move to it as well, off the grouped wmma select.

On an L4, 3 rounds over the site's whole curve (c4–c1024, pf4–pf1024, raw): c16 299 → 500 tok/s (vLLM 442), c8 239 → 276 (vLLM 276), and every other point within ±1.2% apart from two the line does not cause: c4 +3.7%, whose selects keep their kernel, and pf8 −2.3%, which is bimodal on this card. After the rebase, c16 297 → 485 and c8 241 → 280 (2 rounds), and the shipped engine gives c16 301 → 511 and c8 243 → 284. The cost is c24 641.7 against 656.2 (−2.2%, 2 rounds), where the grouped wmma select beat the run kernel at 3 routes an expert; c20 is +1.7%. The alternative, `moe.mxfp4_grouped_reuse = 0`, gave c8 262 and c16 442, and cost c4 13%. Cross-model: gptoss-20b-mini c8 794 → 888 and a 32-expert MiMo-V2.6-Flash miniature c8 463 → 637, c4 309 → 331, the rest within spread. Greedy tokens are identical for raw and pbatch on all three models. At c8/c16 every decode step changes kernel, and outputs diverge further than one engine's reruns do. Against vLLM's greedy tokens at c8/c16, the fix's mean prefix share is 0.24–0.40 against base's 0.30–0.46 (16–32 requests a run). Tests, on both builds: the profile-resolution tests pass (engine-cuda `chip::`, kernels-cuda `tuning`). kernels-cuda `moe_select` passes 5 and fails `the_routed_selects_answer_the_routed_dot` identically on both, at its pipe-kernel case; that failure is pre-existing, and gpt-oss never takes the pipe kernel. engine-cuda's GPU suite, a single-threaded debug build, had passed 20 of 60 on each with no failures at the time of writing.
