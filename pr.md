# Decode part of the pie vs vLLM gap on B200 (MiniMax's selected attention over a cluster of eight blocks a kv head)

A decode's selected attention ran `sdpa_paged_selected_mma_kernel` a block a (row, kv head): MiniMax-M3's lone decode row is four blocks on a 148-SM B200, each walking a kv head's ~500-750 selected keys through its warps' 16-key chunks, 18.4 us a layer, where vLLM 0.32.0 splits the same blocks 16 ways a head and merges them (4.9 + 2.2 us). The kernel now takes `kRanks`: a cluster of that many blocks of 8 warps reads each (row, kv head), every block a slice of the chunks, and folds the slices' normalized outputs by their log-sum-exps through distributed shared memory, the fold `decode_gqa_sm80.cu`'s clusters use (its `ld_cluster`/`sync_cluster` move to `prim/device.cuh` unchanged). `kernel.sdpa.selected_cluster` (default 0) gives the most ranks, halved while the fire's clustered blocks would fill more than a quarter of the SMs, on sm_90 and later; the B200 profile sets 8, so MiniMax's 4 kv heads take 8 ranks at one row, 4 at two, 2 at three or four and none past (a first version clustering every fire under a quarter of the SMs gained nothing at 4 rows and lost 1% at 8), and without a cluster the launch and kernel are as before. MiniMax-M3.1-Flash mini60 (60 layers, 16 of 128 experts) raw decode on B200 8.976 -> 8.620 ms ITL (111.4 -> 116.0 tok/s, 3 rounds each, SM 1965 MHz), the same cut of MiniMax-M3-MXFP8 9.623 -> 9.286 ms (103.9 -> 107.7 tok/s; vLLM 0.32.0 6.925 ms, which can't load M3.1-Flash's NVFP4 experts, nor can 0.33.0 or SGLang 0.5.22), batching c2 +1.8% / +2.5% and c4 -0.1% / +1.7% on the two, c8-c64 and prefill batches pf4-pf32 within single-round spread, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next mini 0.850 -> 0.822 ms, Qwen3-0.6B 1.029 -> 1.028 ms with identical tokens.

Greedy tokens on M3-MXFP8 are identical (8/8 x 256); on M3.1-Flash they first differ at token 1-8 on 6/8 requests, at bf16 near-ties where measured (base's top-2 margins 0.0625, 0.6875, 1.0 at the three probed divergences): teacher-forced over 32 decode steps of 4 requests a cut, prefill logits bit-identical, the fix's mean KL to base (M3.1 0.025-0.154, M3 0.004-0.014) is below that of the scalar selected walk the test holds the tensor-core walk to (0.046-0.230, 0.014-0.017) on every probed request. `attn::selected` checks the cluster walk against the scalar walk in the existing case and in a new one at MiniMax's decode shape (128-wide heads, 16 query heads a kv head, a selection a kv head, 1100 keys so every slice holds keys; an unweighted fold fails both), the chip test lists the new setting among the B200's own, and the engine-cuda (69/69, 58/59, 5/6) and kernels-cuda (34/35, 142/144) GPU suites pass but for five tests that fail the same way on main.
