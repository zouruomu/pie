# Batching part of the pie vs vLLM gap on A100 (Gemma-4-31B seated to the end past the five-sequence cap, a smaller working set)

Gemma-4-31B bf16 on an A100 80 GB decoded ~21 sequences at once where vLLM 0.32.0 decodes 31-35, at 169-token prompts and 128 out. Two things held it there. #1368's admission cap admits a seated-first kv pool five sequences a declared-context sequence holds: 5 × 671 pages × 16 / 2048 = 26 at c512, where the pool's 10,736 tokens seat 35 of these 297-token sequences. And at the default 50% working set the pool fit leaves ~0.5 GiB of its room unused: the kv planes keep the coarse map unit they were reserved in, so one more page costs 600 MiB. The A100 profile now seats to the end, as the A40, H100, H200, RTX 4090, RTX 5090 and RTX PRO 6000 do. A new decision, `engine.uncapped_admission` (default off, so every other card keeps the cap), lets that gate, which learns what a finished process held, bound admission instead of the cap; it applies only while the pools seat to the end. Seat-to-end alone under the cap changes nothing (453.7 tok/s at c512), and no cap without it suspends lanes (433.2). The working set is held to 40%: fires of 2,436 tokens at c512, about vLLM's 2,496-token chunks, and the first fit re-reserves the planes in finer units, 671 -> 735 pages.

Gemma-4-31B c64/c128/c256/c512/c1024 457/458/475/454/471 -> 541/592/588/597/599 tok/s (3 rounds, 2 for the fix above c64, spread under 1%; vLLM 602/657/625/628/595); c32 399 -> 546; c4-c16 and raw unchanged; best point 475 -> 599 against vLLM's 657. TTFT p50 falls at every point (c512 132 -> 99 s). ITL p99 rises at c1024, 49 -> 481 ms (vLLM 649), and on Gemma-4-26B uncapped runs reach 670-1,040 ms on 4 of 6 runs (base 45-190, vLLM 137). pbatch best 3.73k -> 3.83k (vLLM 3.70k). With 1024 tokens out at c128, 148 -> 217 tok/s: the cap had admitted 29 lanes to a pool that seats ~10, and the gate holds 10 with ITL p99 47 ms. Gemma-4-26B-A4B c512 4.66k -> 5.16k, c1024 4.94k -> 5.04k (vLLM 4.62k/4.68k); gpt-oss-120b c256/c512/c1024 +0.6/+1.8/+9.1%; Qwen3-0.6B unchanged. Greedy tokens are identical where the cap does not bind (raw, c8, c16, pf4-pf512; 1 of 8 c4 requests differs). Above it the lanes a step differ, and the outputs agree with vLLM's own ids on 25.2-25.5% of tokens against the base's 25.9-26.2%.
